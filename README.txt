SCULPTED RHYTHM - THE FLOWING GARDEN | INSIDE THE RECEPTION
Zhyar Peshraw Azeez - Salahaddin University, Department of Architecture

OPEN: unzip, then double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Works offline and runs smoothly on any laptop or phone.

RENDER MODE (default) - your own renders, in 3D
 Your three renders (C1, C2, C3) and the stone detail shot are used as they are. A depth map was estimated for each one,
 so when you move the mouse (or tilt your phone) the room shifts in depth, as if you were leaning inside the reception.
 Drag to pan, scroll / pinch to zoom, tap the labels (Light, Tree, Relief, Desk, Water, Stone) for the concept notes.
 "Tour" plays all four renders in sequence.

360 MODE - look in every direction
 "360 around" stands you at the same camera point and lets you look all around, up to the chandelier in the centre of the
 ceiling and back toward the entrance. This part is built from the 3D model (Golden hour / Day / Night).

Keys: 1 2 3 4 view | T tour | L labels | P photo (PNG) | F fullscreen | H help
Depth is approximate (estimated from the flat images), so keep the movement gentle - that is why the range is small.
